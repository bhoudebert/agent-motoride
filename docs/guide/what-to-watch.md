# What to watch

Every plan says where to be careful, with the km mark and the time you get
there: fixed speed cameras, crosswind, low sun in your eyes, cobbles and
gravel, and the share of fast roads.

## Fixed speed cameras

Fixed cameras on or beside the route, from OpenStreetMap, with the km mark,
the leg, the posted limit and the direction. Fixed installations only: mobile
controls are in no map, and an unmapped camera is not shown. The itinerary
presents them as places to watch your speed; check the local rules on camera
information.

## Crosswind

The route is sampled every 2 km with its heading and the time you pass; the
forecast gusts and wind direction give the part blowing across your direction
of travel. Stretches at 35 km/h or more are flagged, 50 km/h or more as
strong. Needs the ride date within 16 days.

## Low sun ahead

The sun's position at each sample's time of passage: a stretch is flagged when
the sun is 0 to 15 degrees above the horizon and within 30 degrees of your
heading. Works for any date, so a long glare stretch on the way home can move
the departure.

## Cobbles and gravel

Every plan reports the km on cobbles or setts and on unpaved roads (gravel,
dirt, compacted), with the stretches by road. The planner avoids them when
another road exists and names the ones that remain.

## Time at 70 km/h or more

Two readings of the rider's own yardstick:

| Figure                | Meaning                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| time on 70+ roads     | share of riding time on roads limited to 70 km/h or more                  |
| time at 70+ estimated | share at an estimated 70 or more, which needs limits of 80 and up (bends) |

## Fast expressways

Roads that are not motorways but are limited to 100 km/h or more: they count
as open road, yet a leisure ride mostly on them is not a motorcycle ride. The
plan shows their share next to the motorway figure, and a leisure ride with a
quarter or more on them goes back to the planner once. See
[what counts as a motorway](/settings#what-counts-as-a-motorway).

## Daylight

Sunrise, sunset, first and last usable light for the ride day, computed for
any date, winter and summer time included. The planner keeps the departure and
the return inside it, and the [morning briefing](/phone-and-gps#the-morning-of-the-ride)
turns a return after dark into a no-go.
