02 / the map

The whole ride on one page

Every roadbook draws its own map: the loop leg by leg, the towns in riding order, where you stop and at what time, and every fixed speed camera with its limit. It shows right in Claude Code or Codex, even on the phone, on the Wi-Fi share page and at the top of the Markdown export.

03 / what it does

A ride, start to finish

From the idea on Thursday night to the roads you rate on Sunday evening. Everything below works from the terminal and from Claude Code or Codex.

1 · BEFORE

  • Plan from one sentence"Saturday, no rain, under 250 km, winding roads." Scouts explore several areas at once.
  • Import a route someone sharedA club's or a friend's GPX or KML, routed so it gets the same figures and checks as your own rides.
  • Start from a photo of a mapA loop drawn on paper or a screenshot: the places on it become the waypoints.
  • Ride a roadbook again"Plan a ride from roadbook 7 on Saturday at 9": the same loop on a new day, with that day's forecast and open stops, no copy.
  • Change a roadbook in words"50 km longer", "leave at 10", "skip Tournai".

2 · THE PLAN

  • Open road firstShare of the ride outside towns, time at 70 km/h or more, slow zones kept small.
  • Stops from your bikeFuel before the tank runs dry, coffee and lunch when you would want them, open on arrival.
  • What to watchFixed speed cameras, crosswind, low sun in your eyes, cobbles and gravel.
  • Checked before you see itCode verifies your limits, no motorways, no repeat of a ride you already have.

3 · RIDE DAY

  • Morning briefingFresh forecast, daylight, traffic at departure, stops still open: go, caution or no-go.
  • The whole ride on one mapThe loop, the towns, your stops and every fixed camera with its limit, as a picture.
  • On the phoneGoogle Maps links that stay on the chosen roads, a whole-ride overview, a page on your Wi-Fi with a QR code.
  • For the GPSA GPX file for Liberty Rider, Kurviger, Garmin or TomTom, stops included.
  • Away from the computerDrive it from the Claude or ChatGPT app with remote control.

4 · BACK HOME

  • Notes from the saddle"Last 10 minutes awesome", "cobbles, never again", said at a stop.
  • Review with your recorded trackNotes land on the road you really rode, detours and pace included.
  • Better next timeLoved roads come back, "never again" roads are avoided, and no ride is proposed twice.
  • Roadbooks and ridesYour loops as roadbooks, rated in a word ("rate roadbook 7 five"); each day you ride one is a ride, listed by date, with its own weather and notes.

05 / how it works

An agent that does the legwork, code that keeps it honest

The model decides where to look and what to propose. Deterministic tools supply every number. The code enforces the rules you set.

Scout

Two to four sub-agents explore candidate regions in parallel: winding roads, a routed loop, the forecast.

Compare

The planner weighs the candidates on open road, slow zones, time at speed, weather and your past rides.

Finish

The chosen loop gets daylight, fixed cameras, fuel and coffee stops placed from your bike's range, and traffic at departure.

Remember

Roadbooks, their ratings and the roads you rated from the saddle shape the next plan. No loop you have is proposed twice.

06 / run it

Three ways in

Same tools, same library, same exports. Pick the one that fits what you already have.

Terminal

A menu and a refine prompt on your Anthropic API key. Full control, validated answers, cost per run.

npm run ride

Claude Code or Codex

The tools plug in as an MCP server. Plan on the subscription you already pay for.

/mcp__ride__plan-ride

From your phone

Leave a Claude Code or Codex session running on a computer or a VPS and drive it from the Claude or ChatGPT app.

claude remote-control
07 / under the hood

Built like something you would rely on

Data
OpenStreetMap roads and limits, Valhalla routing, Open-Meteo forecasts, TomTom traffic.
Time model
Riding time from speed limits and road curvature, not the router's guess.
Rules in code
No motorways unless allowed, no duplicates, rated roads respected, every itinerary checked against your limits, a stuck agent made to answer.
Ride-day check
One command on the morning: fresh forecast, daylight, traffic, stops open on arrival, go or no-go.
Observable
Every session traced and costed, replayable step by step, exportable to any OpenTelemetry tool. Models benchmarked on cost and quality.
Memory
Past sessions teach the next: areas already scouted, roads already found, what you rated. Recalled by place and by words.
Evaluated
Scripted rider requests graded by code, recorded once and replayed free on every change.
Injection-tested
Hostile instructions planted in map names. The rules hold in code whatever the model reads.
Engineering
Specs per capability, tests with simulated services, CI, CodeQL, release automation. TypeScript, SQLite.
08 / try it

Five minutes to a first plan

Full instructions in the README.

$ git clone https://github.com/bhoudebert/agent-motoride && cd agent-motoride
$ npm install
$ cp .env.example .env      # RIDE_HOME, and ANTHROPIC_API_KEY for the terminal mode
$ npm run check             # what is ready, what answers
$ npm run ride              # or open `claude` in this folder
