MotoRide privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026. Applies to the MotoRide app for Android (package com.adequatefunction.motoride).

MotoRide is a motorcycle GPS. It follows a roadbook, warns you of dangerous corners and, if you choose, shares your ride live with people you pick. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics. This page says what leaves your phone, when, and where it goes.

What stays on your phone

  • Your roadbooks and GPX files, the rides MotoRide records (as GPX files), your settings and the saved offline map.
  • Your position while you ride. The corner warnings, turn guidance and voice are worked out on the phone.
  • If you set a server, the access token for it, kept in the phone's secure storage.

The developer does not receive any of this. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Live sharing (off unless you turn it on)

When you share a ride live, the phone sends its position about every 30 seconds to the server you set in the app, which is a server you or someone you trust runs (agentRide's multi-user server). The position is encrypted on the phone (AES-256-GCM) before it is sent. The key is in the link you give to your followers and is never sent to the server, so the server cannot read the position. The followers' web page decrypts it in their browser. Stopping the share ends it. What the server keeps, and for how long, is decided by whoever runs it.

Services MotoRide calls

To search a place, route to it, complete a GPX file with speed limits, roundabouts and camera zones, show the fuel stations and the weather on the way, find places along it, and draw the map, the app calls public open-data services. They receive what is needed for the request, and your IP address, and they have their own privacy policies:

  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap, nominatim.openstreetmap.org): the text of a place you search.
  • Valhalla (valhalla1.openstreetmap.de): the coordinates of a route to a place or of a GPX being completed.
  • Overpass (overpass-api.de, overpass.openstreetmap.fr): the area around a route, to read speed limits, roundabouts and cameras, and to find the fuel stations along it (the route's coordinates, a point every kilometre, once a month for each roadbook you ride), and to find the bakery, café, viewpoint or other kind of place you ask for along the next 60 km (the coordinates of that stretch, when you ask).
  • MET Norway (api.met.no): the weather forecast. The app sends a place, rounded to about a kilometre, for where you are and for every 15 km of the roadbook ahead, up to five places every ten minutes while a ride screen is open. You can turn the weather off on the weather card, and then nothing is sent. Weather data: MET Norway.
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org): the map tiles for the area you look at or save for offline use.

If you set a server and it has a TomTom key, the app also shows the traffic on the next 60 km. Every five minutes it asks that server, and only that server, about seven points of the road ahead (latitude and longitude, here and every 10 km), and the server asks TomTom with its own key. So that server learns roughly where you ride, to about a kilometre: use a server you trust. The app asks the server whether it has traffic each time a ride screen opens (nothing about the ride is sent in that request). You can turn traffic off in the app, and then nothing is sent. Without a server, nothing about traffic exists in the app.

These calls are made only when you use the feature. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Permissions

  • Location, including while the app is in the background during a ride: to follow your position and warn you. It runs as a foreground service with a visible notification.
  • Notifications: the notification shown while a ride runs.
  • Internet: for the services above and live sharing.

What MotoRide does not do

No account or sign-up. No advertising and no advertising identifier. No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service. No sale of data. The app is not directed at children; it is for riders of legal age.

Your choices

Turn live sharing off at any time, remove the server in the app, revoke location permission in Android settings, or uninstall the app. To delete data held on a server you used, ask whoever runs it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions, or a request about your data, can be sent as an issue on github.com/bhoudebert/agent-motoride.
